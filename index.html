Add a song

Free MIDI files (.mid) are easy to find for classical and folk pieces. Choose one and Keystep turns it into falling tiles. Notes from middle C up become right-hand tiles, the rest left-hand.

MusicXML (.musicxml, .xml or .mxl) is what MuseScore, Finale, Sibelius and OMR tools such as Audiveris export, so it keeps the notes exactly. Sheet music scanned with one of those tools comes through cleanly this way.

Checking whether this page can read photos…

Choose a PDF, or a clear straight-on photo or screenshot, of the music. Claude reads the notes into the Type the notes boxes so you can check and fix them before adding. Printed, simple pieces read best.

Write each note as a name and an optional length in beats. C4 is middle C, C4:2 lasts two beats, F#3 and Bb4 work, R:1 is a rest, and C4+E4+G4:2 is a chord. Bar lines (|) are ignored.

Paste them into the Right hand box of another copy of Keystep to move the song there.
